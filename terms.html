Techpoint is open to both individual customers shopping for personal use and businesses looking to purchase electronics in retail or bulk quantities. There is no minimum or maximum order restriction imposed by the Platform itself — order quantities and bulk pricing arrangements are agreed directly between the customer and the vendor.

Techpoint is not limited to Lagos State. While many of our vendors are currently based in Lagos, customers from other Nigerian states are welcome to browse listings and reach out to vendors. Delivery arrangements and coverage outside a vendor's immediate location are agreed directly between the customer and the vendor.

Techpoint does not operate a user account or login system. As a visitor, you are expected to use the Platform respectfully and lawfully at all times. Any contact information you voluntarily provide — for example, when submitting a "Buy Now" inquiry — is used solely for the purposes described in our Privacy Policy below.

While using Techpoint , you must not:

Techpoint is a marketplace platform that connects customers with vendors. Techpoint is not a party to any sale, transaction, or agreement made between a customer and a vendor. Accordingly, Techpoint shall not be liable for the outcome of any transaction, including but not limited to product quality, payment disputes, non-delivery, late delivery, or any other issue arising from a purchase made through contact initiated on the Platform.

The Techpoint website and its services are provided strictly on an "as-is" and "as-available" basis. Returns & Exchange

Our policy for product returns and exchanges

3.1 Return Window

Techpoint does not impose a fixed, platform-wide return window. Each vendor determines their own return window for the products they list. Customers are responsible for confirming the applicable return window directly with the vendor — via WhatsApp or in person — before completing payment for a product.

3.2 Eligible Products

Eligibility for return is determined by each individual vendor based on the specific product, its condition (NEW, USED, or REFURBISHED), and the circumstances of the request. Customers are advised to ask the vendor directly which products and conditions qualify for return before completing a purchase.

3.3 Non-Returnable Products

Whether a product qualifies as non-returnable (for example, due to its condition, type, or nature) is determined by the individual vendor. Customers should clarify any non-returnable conditions with the vendor directly before completing payment.

3.4 Return Conditions

Any conditions that must be met to request a return — such as proof of purchase, product packaging, or contacting the vendor within a specific window — are set by the individual vendor and should be confirmed with them directly.

Techpoint is not involved in, and does not mediate, disputes between customers and vendors regarding returns, refunds, or exchanges. Such matters are strictly between the customer and the vendor to resolve.

3.5 Return Shipping

Responsibility for return shipping costs is at the discretion of the vendor.

3.6 Refund Process

As Techpoint does not process or hold payment for any transaction, refunds — where applicable — are issued directly by the vendor to the customer, at the vendor's discretion. The refund timeframe is agreed directly between the customer and the vendor.

3.7 Exchange Process

Whether a vendor offers product exchanges (for example, swapping for a different item or variant) is entirely at the vendor's discretion. Techpoint does not mandate a formal exchange process; customers should confirm with the vendor directly whether an exchange option is available.

3.8 Vendor Return Obligations

Vendors shall maintain and honor return and refund policies. Where returns are permitted, vendors shall clearly communicate return conditions, honor approved returns, and bear return shipping costs unless otherwise disclosed and permitted by law.
